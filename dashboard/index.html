    Implied value is this entry's own estimate from the snapshot's figures, not a prediction that a token will launch, or what it will be worth if it does. Token odds are the record of similar protocols, not a forecast for this one.

    Methodology

    Implied value = curve(TVL) × category factor × growth factor × age factor × token odds. The size curve is a log-log regression of market cap on TVL, fitted at runtime from the 128 token-trading protocols in the snapshot (ln(mcap) = 6.96 + 0.52 × ln(TVL), R² 0.19): a big protocol carries a lower multiple than a small one. The category factor is the median of (actual mcap / curve-predicted mcap) over the token-trading protocols in the same category, shrunk toward 1.0 and clamped to [0.3, 2.5]; a category counts only with at least 3 trading comps, else no adjustment. The growth factor is 1 + clamp(change_7d, −50, +100) / 100 × 0.5. The age factor is 1 + 0.15 × ln(1 + years since listed_at), capped at 1.5, with the snapshot's median age (1.85 years) where no listing date is recorded. Token odds are the peer-group rate: the share of protocols in the same category, listed within a year and within one decade of TVL, that record a token, shrunk toward the global rate (prior 20). The P25–P75 range is the spread of peer multiples in the row's category.

    Farm economics, one scenario for every row: a $10,000 deposit. The drop pool is 2% of the implied value; the farming pool is 5% of TVL and the farmer's share is the deposit over it; fees are one gas transaction per farm step that names gas: $5.00 Ethereum, $0.01 Solana, $3.00 Bitcoin, $0.25 elsewhere; time is the steps' stated minutes, each counted once. Net = modelled drop × token odds − fees. FARM means net over $750 and over $500 an hour — the top decile of modelled nets under this scenario (the median row nets $322). SKIP means net under $100, under 1% of the deposit. The verdict ranks farming, not launch value: the biggest launches are the worst farms, because a $10,000 stake is a drop in their TVL.

    How good is the model? The evidence strip above gives the held-out numbers: the curve beats a flat multiple on both leave-one-out and walk-forward tests. Token odds score 0.224 (Brier) against 0.248 for the base rate — they discriminate weakly, so they are a rough guide, not a prediction. The curve explains about a fifth of the variation in market caps, which is why the value ranges are wide.

    Opportunity cost: every farm box carries a T-bill line — the same deposit in a US 3-month Treasury bill over the 30-day minimum hold the farm plans ask for, at the rate and source date in the box. A farm has to beat the risk-free rate, not just its fees.

    Sliders and the matrix: the sliders above the board recompute net and verdict live from the same formulas — the board's verdict is a scenario, not a fact. The detail matrix shows the same formula at $1,000 / $10,000 / $50,000 and 1% / 2% / 5% drop shares.

    Coverage: the board holds every protocol in the snapshot that records no token — all 40 watchlist protocols (TVL ≥ $50M, no token recorded, not a wrapped, staked or bridged product) plus 158 more. A protocol is here because its DefiLlama record carries no token — no gecko id, no symbol, no market cap — which is not proof it has none. Of the 758 protocols, 416 already record a token and 144 are excluded by the watchlist rule (bridges, staking, CEX products, versioned or wrapped names).
