    Implied value is this entry's own estimate from the snapshot's figures, not a prediction that a token will launch, or what it will be worth if it does. Token odds are the record of similar protocols, not a forecast for this one.

    Methodology

    Implied value = curve(TVL) × category factor × growth factor × age factor × token odds. The size curve is a log-log regression of market cap on TVL, fitted at runtime from the 128 token-trading protocols in the snapshot (ln(mcap) = 6.96 + 0.52 × ln(TVL), R² 0.19): a big protocol carries a lower multiple than a small one, so a $2.75B protocol is not priced at 275× a $10M one. The category factor is the median of (actual mcap / curve-predicted mcap) over the token-trading protocols in the same category, shrunk toward 1.0 and clamped to [0.3, 2.5]; a category counts only with at least 3 trading comps, else no adjustment. The growth factor is 1 + clamp(change_7d, −50, +100) / 100 × 0.5. The age factor is 1 + 0.15 × ln(1 + years since listed_at), capped at 1.5, with the snapshot's median age (1.85 years) standing in where no listing date is recorded. Token odds are the peer-group rate: the share of protocols in the same category, listed within a year and within one decade of TVL, that record a token, shrunk toward the global rate with a prior weight of 20. The P25–P75 value range comes from the spread of peer multiples in the row's category.

    Farm economics, one scenario for every row: a $10,000 deposit. The drop pool is 2% of the implied value; the farming pool is 5% of TVL and the farmer's share is the deposit over it; fees are one transaction per farm step that names gas, at $5.00 on Ethereum, $0.01 on Solana, $3.00 on Bitcoin and $0.25 on every other chain; time is the steps' stated minutes, each counted once. Net = modelled drop × token odds − fees. FARM means net over $750 and over $500 an hour — the top decile of modelled nets under this scenario (the median row nets $322). SKIP means net under $100, under 1% of the deposit. The verdict ranks farming, not launch value: the biggest launches are the worst farms, because a $10,000 stake is a drop in their TVL.

    How good is the model? Held out, the size curve misses by ×3.4 (leave-one-out) and ×3.1 (walk-forward, fitted on tokens listed before 2025-01-01 and tested on later ones), against ×5.1 and ×5.4 for a flat median multiple. Token odds score 0.224 (Brier) against 0.248 for the base rate — they discriminate weakly, so they are a rough guide, not a prediction. The curve explains about a fifth of the variation in market caps, which is why the value ranges are wide.

    Coverage: the board holds every protocol in the snapshot that records no token — all 40 watchlist protocols (TVL ≥ $50M, no token recorded, not a wrapped, staked or bridged product) plus all 158 more. A protocol is on this board because its DefiLlama record carries no token — neither a gecko id, nor a symbol, nor a market cap — which is not proof it has none. Of the 758 protocols in the snapshot, 416 already record a token and 144 are excluded by the watchlist rule (bridges, staking, CEX products, versioned or wrapped names).
