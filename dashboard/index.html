    Implied value is an estimate from the snapshot's figures, not a prediction that a token will launch. Token odds are the record of similar protocols, not a forecast.

    Methodology

    Implied value = curve(TVL) × category factor × growth factor × token odds. The curve is a log-log regression of market cap on TVL, fitted at runtime from the 128 token-trading protocols; the category factor is the median (actual / curve-predicted mcap) in the same category, shrunk toward 1.0 (n/(n+64)), clamped to [0.3, 2.5], 3+ comps; growth is 1 + clamp(change_7d, −50, +100) / 100 × 0.5. Age was tested and is not in the value — it raises the leave-one-out miss — so it enters only through token odds: a logistic on years listed, ln TVL, 7-day growth and a family adjustment (each category's shrunk residual token rate), fitted on all 758 protocols; coefficients ± s.e. are in any detail view, and the P25–P75 range is the spread of the model's own leave-one-out errors. Farm economics are in any detail view: $10,000 deposit, drop pool 2% of implied value, farming pool 5% of TVL, net = modelled drop × token odds − fees. Every term was tested held out on two tests (leave-one-out and walk-forward, fitted before 2025, tested on later listings) and stays only if it lowers the miss or Brier on both; two shuffled-label controls must not help, and do not. Coverage: every protocol in the snapshot that records no token — all 40 watchlist protocols plus 158 more — because its DefiLlama record carries no gecko id, symbol or market cap, which is not proof it has none.

    Data: sealed DefiLlama snapshot, 2026-11-14. Values are estimates, not predictions.
